# Where I want to take Ayah Grammar

The reader, word-study panel and 500-verb library are the foundation. My next priorities are:

- **Review the learning data.** Check difficult roots and printed book anomalies with Arabic/Bangla reviewers, while keeping corrections traceable to the source.
- **Make saved words useful for daily practice.** Add review sessions grouped by root, followed by spaced repetition.
- **Make grammar explanations easier to follow.** Expand the short guides for aspect, voice, mood and weak verbs.
- **Give readers control over offline storage.** Add chapter packs with visible download size, progress and removal controls.
- **Improve accessibility on real devices.** Continue keyboard, screen-reader, Arabic/Bangla rendering and PWA checks across phones and tablets.

These are planned improvements, not features already available in the app.
